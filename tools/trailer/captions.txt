# The words of the trailer, read by post.py and set in Minecraft's own font. TrailerCoverageTest keeps this in step with the
# shots of client/ShowcaseRun. The order and length of the full cut are in edit.txt; the captions read as one story in that order.
#
# title, subtitle        the opening card (under the logo); the full cut opens instead on the title_sequence shot, with the title
#                        over it and that shot's caption as the line under it
# outro, outro_sub       the closing card (post.py adds the version line from gradle.properties)
# outro_links         the line under that telling viewers the download links are in the video description
# outro_notice           the AI notice at the foot of the closing card
# ai_docs                the line under the AI notice on the vertical cut's end piece, saying where to read how AI is used
# ai_docs_url            the address of that page (docs/AI.md), under the AI notice in both video descriptions
# vertical_ai_tag        the short AI notice at the top of the vertical cut from its first frame, for a viewer who leaves early
# vertical_description   the first line of the Shorts description (post.py writes run/trailer/description_vertical.txt)
# caption <shot>         the line shown over that shot in the full trailer (every shot has one; a `quiet` piece of edit.txt
#                        does not show its own, the one before stays up)
# short_shots            the shots of the teaser, in order, three seconds each (the vertical cut has its own edit, vertical.txt)
# short_caption <shot>   the few big words over each of those
# thumbnail              the shot the thumbnail is taken from
# vertical_thumbnail     the shot the 9:16 thumbnail (Shorts) is taken from, vertical_thumbnail_at seconds into it; vertical_title is its two big lines split by |, vertical_sub the small one
# description            the pitch at the top of the video description (post.py writes run/trailer/description.txt with every full cut)
# link <name>            a line of the description's Links list: "link GitHub: https://..." (add the Modrinth and CurseForge pages here
#                        once their addresses are fixed; post.py never guesses them)
# chapter <shot>         a chapter: the gold line over its captions, a flash and a hit where it starts in the cut, and a chapter of the description starting at that shot (a shot a run lacks is skipped; chapters under ten seconds are folded into the one before)
# Keep captions to one line (about 60 characters at 1080p), plain ASCII (the font's own characters), and true: say only what
# the mod does.
