# The edit of the vertical cut (9:16, for YouTube Shorts and the like). post.py reads it; TrailerCoverageTest checks that every
# shot it names exists, that every piece is a whole number of half bars, that every piece but the last has words, and that
# the last is the end piece. It is cut for a feed, where a viewer decides in the first second whether to stay:
#
# - no title card at the start: it opens full frame (no `fit` band) tight on a barking exhaust mouth and pulls back fast
#   to the whole engine revving (`exhaust_mouth`), with words on the first frame;
# - every piece on screen at least 3 s, long enough to read its words and see what it shows, and the whole cut at most 90 s,
#   inside the length limit of Shorts and TikTok;
# - the engines heard, not only seen (`engine`), as most viewers have the sound on in a feed but many do not, so the words
#   carry the story alone too;
# - every word, the end piece's too, kept above the bottom 28% (y 1380 of 1920) and off the right edge, where the apps
#   draw their description and buttons;
# - the end piece lays the title, the links and the AI notice over moving footage, and the cut ends there with no fade, so
#   it loops cleanly back into the opening.
#
#   <shot> <seconds> [at=<s>] [speed=<x>] [x=<0..1>] [fit] [zoom=<1..3>] [flash] [engine] [cold] [end] | <WORDS>
#
# seconds   how long the piece runs, a multiple of 1.5 (half a bar at 80 beats a minute), so every cut falls on the beat
# at=       where in the shot the piece starts, in seconds of the shot (default: the middle of the shot)
# speed=    plays the shot faster
# x=        where across the 16:9 shot the 9:16 window sits, 0 the left edge, 1 the right (default 0.5, the middle)
# fit       the whole 16:9 picture in a band across the middle, on a blurred copy of itself, for a subject too wide to crop
# zoom=     with fit: takes a window that much smaller into the band (a little above the middle), for a small subject
# flash     a white flash at the cut
# engine    the music drops and the game's sound comes up
# cold      game sound only, no music
# end       the end piece: the title, the links and the AI notice over the footage. Last, and only once.
# WORDS     the few big words over the piece, on two lines: a small white one, then a big gold one. A `/` says where the
#           gold line starts; without one it is the last word
#
# A shot may be used more than once (a long shot split so its words can change half-way). A shot that is not in this run
# (the Aeronautics shots without Sable) is left out.
