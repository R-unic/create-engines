What 2.0.1 is, from `git diff v2.0.0..HEAD` (the changelog is `## Changelog for 2.0.1` in [LISTING.md](../LISTING.md)):

- Sable's own air pressure drives thin air when Sable is installed; the height curve is now tunable (`altitudeStart`,
  `altitudeScale`, `altitudeFloor`) and its floor moves 0.2 to 0.25.
- Pollution: `pollutionRate` default 1.0 to 0.25; new `carbonRate`, `sulfurRate`, `pollutionAreaCap` (0, off) and
  `pollutionEngineCeiling` (0, off).
- Fixes for engines on Create Aeronautics structures (pistons froze: culling) and for the crank drifting out of line with
  Create's shafts (Flywheel clock; new access transformer line).
- Create: Diesel Generators fluids in the common fuel tags (optional entries).
- A Ponder page for the Heavy Starter.
- Turbocharger: the wheel's energy is held to one atmosphere over the boost limit, and a non-finite wheel is stopped (a big or
  stacked engine could run the boost to tens of bar, then NaN).
- Fuel fire: liquid fuel lying in the world burns when fire, lava, magma or a lit campfire is beside it; `compat.fuelFire`
  (default on) turns it off.
