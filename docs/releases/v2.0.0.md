- **Battery depth (2026-11-19, branch `2.0-battery-depth`, uncommitted until the author says).** The ROADMAP item "Battery depth (2.0)",
  author's decision 2026-11-19 ("all five", and cold-weather charge on by default). Numbers and reasons are in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
  (1) **Pooled banks:** batteries that touch are one bank (`BatteryBank`, found by walking over touching batteries, not stored); capacity is
  the sum, draws and charges are shared in proportion; an engine or alternator touching one member reaches all of them (the scanner adds the
  whole bank to `layout.batteries()` and to the engine's members, so blueprints record it). A lone battery is a bank of one.
