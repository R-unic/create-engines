## What changed since v1.4.0 (from `git diff v1.4.0..2.0`, 34 files, 2204 insertions, 250 deletions)

- **TorqueSource seam (preview API).** `EngineSimulation` drives, rubs, heats and oils anything that implements
  `TorqueSource` (`engine/sim/TorqueSource.java`); `CylinderModel` implements it. A rotary chamber is now a `CycleDrive`
  (piston or rotary implementation) instead of a flag tested on every step. Design note: `docs/design/ENGINE_SEAM.md`.
  The mod ships no extra source. `compat/behaviour.txt` and `compat/trajectories.txt` are untouched.
- **Packs and addons can add sources.** `api/EngineExtensions.register(EngineSourceProvider)`; `EngineController` asks
  every provider when it builds an engine. `EngineExtensions.rebuild` rebuilds the engine at a block keeping motion,
  oil and heat. A provider that throws or returns null is reported and skipped. Providers can watch blocks, so placing
  or breaking one rebuilds the engines in reach. Added sources get boost (`setIntake`, `Induction`), and their
  `status()` lines show on the crankshaft goggles (`EngineSummary`, `StatusLine`, `GoggleOverlayLayout`,
  `GoggleOverlayMixin`, mixin config entry). Marked preview, not frozen until 2.0.
- **Precision head models redrawn (visible change).** The plug, injector and injector-plug of the precision head no
  longer z-fight with its cam covers and bolts (`block_injector`, `block_plug`, `block_injector_plug` under
  `precision_cylinder_head`, from `tools/generate_models.py`). The plug is 1.5 px taller and the injector has a small
  terminal cap. The standard head is unchanged. New guard: `PrecisionHeadZFightTest`.
- **Performance, behaviour unchanged.** No per-tick starter list or carried-motion capture when nothing changed
  (`EngineController`, `EngineTopology`); engine part visuals skip posing when outside the view frustum; camshaft
  lobes pose without a per-frame float array.
- **Ratchet.** `src/smoke/mutation-ratchet.txt` lowered 172 to 170 (improvement, measured by the author's `pitest`
  run at 2680e03; not re-measured here).
- New tests: `TorqueSourceTest`, `CycleDriveTest`, `StatusLineTest`, `GoggleOverlayLayoutTest`,
  `PrecisionHeadZFightTest`, and GameTests in `PackToolsGameTests` (with `IdleSource`).

No registry ID, key, config value or data-map format is added, renamed or removed in this diff (`src/main` has no new
`compat/manifest.txt` lines). Still to confirm on the final tree.
