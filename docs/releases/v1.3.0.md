## Exceptions

- 60-minute soak not built (known exception from STABILITY.md 2.4).
- Behaviour changes to existing factories in 1.3.0 (all named in the changelog), with who approved each:
  - Basin ethanol and biodiesel quantities: approved by the author in the 1.3 decisions, 2026-11-16.
  - Ethanol pre-ignition point under boost (5.0 instead of its 3.4 autoignition point; optional `preignition_temperature`
    fuel data-map field). Moves guarded numbers: only boosted spark builds on ethanol, only the top speeds of the curve;
    gasoline, other fuels and every other speed are byte-identical. Top-speed torque: 1 cylinder crude 0.264 to 0.494,
    6 cylinder crude 1.211 to 2.076, 1 cylinder injector 0.503 to 0.732, 6 cylinder injector 4.058 to 5.716, 1 cylinder
    precise 1.153 to 1.206, 6 cylinder precise 5.905 to 6.187. Guards moved by hand in d5db936: `behaviour.txt` (6 lines),
    `trajectories.txt` (seeds 1 and 28), `manifest.txt` (`fuel.json` hash). Approved by the author, by name, in chat on
    2026-11-16.
  - Two engines on one kinetic network no longer hold each other turning (`EngineController.drivenFromOutside`; a starter
    also stops cranking while its engine is split). Approved by the author in chat on 2026-11-17.
  - Other changed behaviour in d5db936: machine-loop and engine-note voice limits (16 loops, two-second rest, four starts a
    second), quiet loops start at 0.05 instead of 0.02, the gas chamber is cut down from 16 blocks and not drawn past
    40, the new pre-ignition knock sound replaces the metal tick, goggle wording shortened in 11 places. Approved by the
    author in chat on 2026-11-17. None of these is covered by a guard.
- **Heap: cause found 2026-11-17, accepted by the author ("keep styles, ship +440 MB").** The new `casing_style` property
  (3 values, unreleased until 1.3) on the five pipe blocks (intake manifold, exhaust manifold, heat exchanger, throttle,
  MAF sensor) triples each pipe's block states, and each state carries the 64-way connection multipart; a heap
  histogram showed 7.4 million `KeyValueCondition` lambdas from vanilla's multipart loader. Dropping the property on
  pipes only (private copy, not applied) took the settled heap from 1267 MB to 830 MB (pre-style 752 MB). The author
  chose to keep riveted and louvred cladding on pipes, so the number stays: players pay about 440 MB more RAM at model
  load, and `casing_style` on pipes becomes part of the 1.x promise. Revisit for 1.4 (cheaper pipe models) or 2.0.
