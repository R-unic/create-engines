## Exceptions

- 60-minute soak not built (known exception from STABILITY.md 2.4).
- **Two transmission GameTests were removed from the suite (approved below).** `neutralWithAFastSourceOnTheOutputKeepsTheTransmission` (a 200 RPM source on the output side) and
  `neutralWhileTheEngineSlowsWithASourceOnTheOutputKeepsTheTransmission` (a 64 RPM source, engine slowing) were in `EngineGameTests.java` in the working tree and failing in a private copy ("Destroyed before the
  shift: shaft1": Create broke the shaft beside the transmission before the shift to neutral); then, between that run and the next, they were deleted from the working tree by another Claude session (the "Transmission
  shift to neutral stall" session is the likely one) along with a `RIG SPEEDS` debug print, and committed with the rest in b69428f. No code under `content/transmission` or the engine changed to make them pass. **This is a guard
  removed, not a bug shown to be fixed.** What is and is not covered now: a transmission with a slow source (16 RPM, either direction) or a 200 RPM reverse source on its output, the stalled-engine and two-controller
  cases are still tested; a 200 RPM forward source and the 64 RPM stalling case are not. Decide whether those two scenarios are supposed to work (then they must be fixed in the code and the tests restored) or are
  unsupported (then say so on the listing and keep this line).
- **Author decision 2026-11-18 (in chat): the removal of the two transmission tests was intended** (the author first said they thought so, then that it looked like they were meant to go). Those two scenarios are unsupported, and the 1.4.0 changelog in
  `docs/LISTING.md` now says so under "Known limit". The removal is an approved exception, not a fix.
- **Approved by the author 2026-11-18 (in chat): behaviour changes to existing factories in 1.4.0,** both named in the changelog:
  - An Oil Sump's oil exchange no longer wears plant oil (or any non-engine-oil lubricant) into used oil (commit b17204d, "Stop plant oil turning into used oil in the oil exchange"). Engine oil is unchanged. Guarded
    numbers did not move (`behaviour.txt` and `trajectories.txt` are unchanged); the sump has no baseline entry.
  - A refining column keeps catalyst across a controller move (it used to be stranded in the old controller), shows "wrong catalyst", and the Reboiler's catalyst slot message and sound. Column refining numbers are unchanged.
- **Heap: the hoped 440 MB was not recovered.** Sharing one baked model per multipart block saved 106 MB (1269 to 1163 MB settled). The rest is the block states themselves (about 151,000 `BlockState` objects with
  their property tables and occlusion shapes; a pipe has one per connection shape, encasing, casing style and dye), found by a class histogram. Cutting it means removing a state property, and `casing_style` and the dye are in the 1.x promise,
  so it moves to 2.0 (`docs/ROADMAP.md`). The load-time peak is not reduced. The same histogram showed about 54 million queued chunk-save tasks in the audit's walking phase. Investigated 2026-11-17 with 15 histograms of the audit JVM (9 `IOWorker` objects, no queued saves): not reproduced, and the earlier reading was most likely taken from a different JVM (not proven).
- JEI page never run in a game: JEI 19.70.0.469 needs NeoForge 21.1.238 and this build runs 21.1.219 (`docs/COMPAT.md`); the author reported the refining page working in their own world on 2026-11-17. The new fuel and part pages have
  not been seen in JEI at all. EMI ran (matrix row, 1062 checks).
- The mutation ratchet file `src/smoke/mutation-ratchet.txt` is 172 where v1.3.0 had 177; it was lowered in the 1.4 work before this record. Measured here: PIT on `engine.sim` at f1c04bd killed 1729, 170 survived,
  2 have no test reaching them, so 172, which is the limit. (`engine.sim` gained `PartStats`.) The coverage floor is unchanged at 0.15 and now has a margin of 0.0008.
